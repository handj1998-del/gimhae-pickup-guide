Gimhae Airport Pick-up Guide

Gimhae Airport Branch · Before Your Rental

1Pick-up Location

Domestic Terminal 1F (Arrivals)
Our counter is located between Gate 2 and Gate 3.
About a 5-minute walk from the International Terminal.

2What to Bring (Originals only)

  • Passport – for identification
  • A valid International Driving Permit (IDP) – please check its expiry date and that it covers the right vehicle category:
    • Category B: vehicles with up to 8 seats, not counting the driver's seat
    • Category D: vehicles with more than 8 seats, not counting the driver's seat

3Before You Arrive

Please tell us your arrival flight number (e.g., KE1234) by replying to our email.

⚡ Fast Pick-up Service

Send us scanned copies of your passport and International Driving Permit in advance for a quick and easy pick-up!
